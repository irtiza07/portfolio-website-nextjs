Although I entered 2025 with the mindset of “no new credit cards”, some of the smaller sign-up-bonuses were too tempting to turn down. 

The goal this quarter is to hit the minimum spending requirement for 2 new credit cards (more on them below), while maximizing earning Chase Ultimate Rewards and Bilt points.

Let’s get started.

![](https://ds0fmzhunbzyk.cloudfront.net/credit-cards-photos/freedom_flex.png)

## Chase Freedom Flex

I still have 2 months left on the "5X Gas and Grocery for your first 12 months" new card bonus on my <ReferralLink card="chaseFreedomFlex">Chase Freedom Flex</ReferralLink>.

Even better, one of the rotating 5X quarterly this quarter is Grocery. 

That means, I am essentially getting 9X on all my grocery purchases this quarter. That’s an opportunity I cannot miss\! 

So, I am putting all my gas and grocery spend on my <ReferralLink card="chaseFreedomFlex">Chase Freedom Flex</ReferralLink> to earn Ultimate Rewards points. 

![](https://ds0fmzhunbzyk.cloudfront.net/credit-cards-photos/fidelity-signature-rewards.png)

## Fidelity Rewards Visa Signature

One of the new cards (and easily one of my newest favorites) I acquired recently is the Fid[elity Rewards Visa Signature](https://www.fidelity.com/spend-save/visa-signature-card) credit card. It’s an unlimited 2X no foreign transaction fee VISA card with a high credit limit. 

I will get a $150 bonus after spending $1,000 in the first 3 months. Quite easy\!

Until I hit the minimum spending requirement, I will put all my purchases (except grocery and gas) on [this card](https://www.fidelity.com/spend-save/visa-signature-card).

![](https://ds0fmzhunbzyk.cloudfront.net/credit-cards-photos/amex-blue-business-plus.png)

## American Express Blue Business Plus

Similarly, I also got myself an [AMEX Blue Business Plus](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/referral/business/bluebusinessplus-credit-card?CORID=M~D~I~R~T~H~s~U~4~K-1793844677309-112875&GENCODE=349993340941547&extlink=US-MGM-INAV-copypaste-1110-201279-K44D%3A9933&ref=MDIRTHsU4K&xl=cp15) in December 2024, albeit with a higher sign up bonus. 

After spending $6,000 in the first 4 months, I will receive 25,000 Membership Rewards points. That’s somewhere between $500 and $750 in travel value.

I already had a [Blue Business Plus](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/referral/business/bluebusinessplus-credit-card?CORID=M~D~I~R~T~H~s~U~4~K-1793844677309-112875&GENCODE=349993340941547&extlink=US-MGM-INAV-copypaste-1110-201279-K44D%3A9933&ref=MDIRTHsU4K&xl=cp15) (BBP), but this was a no-lifetime-language (NLL) target offer that I couldn’t turn down. I plan on canceling my old BBP, and making this one my new “catch-all” card. 

Once I receive the sign-up-bonus from my Fidelity Rewards card, I will start spending on the Blue Business Plus. 

Putting $6,000 purchases on the [BBP](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/referral/business/bluebusinessplus-credit-card?CORID=M~D~I~R~T~H~s~U~4~K-1793844677309-112875&GENCODE=349993340941547&extlink=US-MGM-INAV-copypaste-1110-201279-K44D%3A9933&ref=MDIRTHsU4K&xl=cp15) will net me 37,000 MR points (valued between $740 to $1,110). That’s somewhere in the ballpark of 12% to 19% return on spend. 

![](https://ds0fmzhunbzyk.cloudfront.net/credit-cards-photos/bilt.png)

## Bilt Mastercard

The <ReferralLink card="biltMastercard">Bilt card</ReferralLink> wraps up my Q1'2025 credit card setup.

The card gives me 1X on rent. No other card gives me points on rent, without a 2-3% credit card fee. 

To successfully receive the rent points, I need to put 5 transactions on <ReferralLink card="biltMastercard">this card</ReferralLink> every month. I can hit that without too much hassle. Every week, I have recurring donations set up to 2 different non-profit organizations.

So, <ReferralLink card="biltMastercard">Bilt</ReferralLink> ends up becoming my "rent+donation" card every month.
